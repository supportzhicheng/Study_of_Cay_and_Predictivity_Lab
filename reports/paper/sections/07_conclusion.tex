\section{Conclusion}

The project separates historical replication quality from updated empirical
evidence and records the distinction in generated tables, captions, and the
replication audit. The resulting CAY Analysis Suite provides a cleaned
quarterly panel, an independently estimated trend deviation, reproducible
forecasting regressions, and original diagnostics through \UpdatedSampleEnd.
The current historical audit status is \texttt{\OverallReplicationStatus}; any
remaining failed checks identify data-definition or implementation questions
that must be resolved rather than hidden. Extension analyses are intentionally
excluded from this report.
