\section{Our Product: CAY Lab}
\label{sec:our_product_cay_lab}

CAY Lab exposes decomposition choice, sample range, training window,
predictive horizon, and risky-asset target while preserving the project's
econometric definitions. The root workflow records those choices and consumes
only declared data caches.

\subsection{Worked Example: Wealth Groups and QQQ}

The worked example uses Federal Reserve Distributional Financial Accounts
wealth-group detail and a declared QQQ adjusted-close cache. It forecasts
two-quarter QQQ log returns with an eight-quarter rolling training window for
the bottom 50 percent, middle 40 percent, and top 10 percent wealth groups.
The generated manifest records the segment, page, sample, target, label, and
QQQ source hash for each figure.
