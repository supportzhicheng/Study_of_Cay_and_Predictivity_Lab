\begin{abstract}
This project reconstructs the consumption--wealth trend deviation $cay$,
reproduces the principal empirical results of Lettau and Ludvigson (2001),
and updates the evidence through \UpdatedSampleEnd. The report separates
historical replication quality from updated empirical findings, documents the
underlying data sources and construction choices, and adds original diagnostics
for the macroeconomic inputs.
\end{abstract}
